Bounding-box steering · Acro

Each change in box X/Y drives pitch and roll through the selected steering response. Faster box movement requests a faster turn, up to the profile’s rate limit. A stationary box requests no turn, even off-centre. At the centre, turning stops and the drone holds its current attitude. Thrust, gravity and drag continue to affect its motion.

Compare the four modes with the same box position, centre timer and throttle. The lime trail shows the simulated flight path. Overview fits the whole path; Follow stays close to the drone. Drag to orbit; scroll to zoom.

· Approximate flight physics: thrust/weight 4, drag-limited speed 40 m/s, initial launch speed 12 m/s. Profiles control bounding-box steering; this is not a calibrated prediction of your airframe.
