m
Type the points

One point per line: latitude, longitude (decimal degrees). At least 3.

A saved geofence is always on. In a WASP flight, leaving it starts SAFEDOWN at once: it levels, flies back to the GO spot and lands. ARM is refused while the drone is outside it or has no GPS position. Draw: drag a finger around the area, or tap the corners. Without GPS the map still works: Make puts the circle at the map centre.

Seeker missions: heading for the edge closer than the geofence margin, or leaving it, stops the mission and flies home (RTL) with an alarm on this phone. Every mission needs a geofence and checks its route against it before ARM.

    The cue comes over the RFD radio (asterix062_bridge.py --rfd-dev on the laptop sends the followed track as $RFD lines) or as CAT062 over UDP. SEEK climbs and turns to it, follows it in GUIDED at the speed above with the nose on it and stops short of it. A new target on the feed asks to switch. When the camera holds the target it stops and asks: YES = ALT_HOLD and the AI (AUTO if AUTO is on), NO = wait here for another target. ABORT or the geofence: RTL. The pilot's mode switch takes over at any time.
